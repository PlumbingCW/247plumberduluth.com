Planning a project outside an emergency

Most of our calls are urgent, but many are planned. If you want a water heater moved, a bathroom plumbed or a line replaced, call during the day and tell us your address and what you want to do. We confirm coverage, talk through the options and give you a price before we schedule the work.

Planned jobs let us bring the right parts the first time and choose a time that fits your week. They also give you room to compare options, so you are not deciding with water on the floor.

If you manage several properties, tell us on the first call. We can note the addresses, the shutoff locations and the best contact for each building, so the next visit starts faster.

What does not change by neighborhood

Wherever you live, the same things matter in a plumbing emergency. Know where the main water valve is, keep the shutoff to the water heater in mind and keep our number handy. The Gwinnett County Department of Water Resources supplies water and runs the public sewer across this whole area, and its rules about who owns which part of the line are the same from one street to the next.

The owner is responsible for the private sewer lateral and for the water line from the meter to the house. The county handles the meter and the public mains. That split is the first thing we explain on a visit, because it decides who pays for the repair.
