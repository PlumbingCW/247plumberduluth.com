What to tell us

A few details help us send the right plumber with the right parts.

Your address and ZIP code

We confirm that you are in our area and plan the route.

What you see, hear or smell

Water, sewage, gas, a noise or a drip all tell us something.

Whether water is still running

If so, shut off the main valve and tell us.

The age and type of the equipment

A water heater brand and age helps us bring parts.

When it started

A sudden problem and a slow one are handled differently.

The best number to reach you

We may call back from the road.

Emergency or planned work

For a burst pipe, a sewage backup, an overflowing toilet or a failed water heater, call right away. We begin by helping you stop the damage and we talk through timing.

For planned work such as an installation or a repipe, call during the day to talk through the options and pick a time. We give a price before we begin.

If you smell gas

Leave the building at once, do not use switches or phones inside and call 911 or the gas utility from a safe distance. A gas leak is not a plumbing emergency to handle yourself. Call us after the area is safe if a plumbing repair is needed.
