Frozen Pipes in Duluth, GA: How to Prevent and Thaw Them

Georgia winters are mild, which is why a hard freeze catches homes off guard. Know where pipes freeze, how to prevent it and what to do when water stops or a pipe bursts.

Why pipes freeze in a mild climate

Pipes freeze when the temperature around them stays below freezing long enough for the water inside to turn to ice. In the South, many homes have pipes in places that northern builders would insulate, such as exterior walls, vented crawlspaces, garages and attics.

The pressure of expanding ice splits the pipe, but the leak starts only when the ice thaws. That is why a pipe can look fine in the morning and flood in the afternoon.

Prevent freezing before the cold arrives

The National Weather Service and utilities give similar advice for a hard freeze.

  • 1Disconnect and drain garden hoses and cover outdoor faucets, or install frost-proof hose bibs.
  • 2Insulate pipes in the attic, the garage, the crawlspace and exterior walls with foam sleeves.
  • 3Seal gaps where pipes or cables enter the house to keep cold air out.
  • 4Open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls so warm air reaches the pipes.
  • 5Let a faucet drip, cold and hot, on the coldest nights, preferably on the pipes farthest from the meter.
  • 6Keep the heat on and set no lower than 55 degrees if you travel.

If water stops, thaw carefully

A pipe that is frozen but not burst can be thawed safely.

  • 1Open the faucet. Leave the tap open so water can flow as the ice melts and so pressure can escape.
  • 2Find the frozen section. Look for frost, a bulge or a pipe that is cold to the touch.
  • 3Apply gentle heat. Use a hair dryer, a heating pad or warm towels, starting near the faucet and working toward the freeze.
  • 4Never use flame. Do not use a torch, a kerosene heater or an open flame, which can start a fire or ruin the pipe.
  • 5Check for leaks. When water flows, inspect the pipe and the joints for cracks.
  • 6Call us if it will not thaw. If you cannot find the freeze, or you see a crack, call a plumber.

If a pipe bursts

Shut off the main water valve immediately, turn off power to wet areas if it is safe and open faucets to drain the lines. Take photos for insurance and call us. We repair the break, restore water and tell you which areas to dry out.

Learn more on our burst pipe repair page, and see water line repair if the break is on the line from the meter.

Which pipes freeze first

Pipes in exterior walls, in unheated garages and attics, and in vented crawlspaces freeze first. So do pipes that run along a north wall and pipes close to a gap where cold air leaks in, such as a dryer vent, a cable hole or a foundation vent.

Hose bibs and sprinkler backflow devices are other common failures. They sit outside, and a freeze can crack their brass or plastic bodies. The leak shows up in spring when you first turn them on.

Before you leave on a winter trip

If you travel during a cold spell, keep the heat on at 55 degrees or higher and ask a neighbor to check the house. Open cabinet doors on exterior walls and leave a faucet dripping. Consider shutting off the main valve and draining the lines if the trip is long.

After a freeze, walk through the house and look for wet spots, stains and low pressure. A pipe can leak after the thaw, so check twice.

What to do if the cold lasts several days

Long cold spells are harder on pipes than a single night. Keep the heat on, keep faucets dripping and check the pipes in the garage and attic each day. If the forecast is severe, shut off water to outdoor spigots and know where the main valve is before you need it. If pipes freeze repeatedly in one spot, the fix is usually insulation or rerouting, and a plumber can look at the run.

After a freeze, check these things

Damage can show up hours or days after the thaw.

  • 1Walk the house and look for wet spots, stains and sagging ceilings.
  • 2Listen for running water with every fixture off.
  • 3Check the water meter and see if it is moving.
  • 4Open each faucet and watch for weak flow, which can mean a partial freeze.
  • 5Inspect the water heater, the hose bibs and any pipes in the garage.

Insurance and records

If a pipe bursts, photograph the damage before cleanup, keep receipts for emergency repairs and call your insurer early. Many policies cover sudden burst pipes, but some exclude damage if the house was unheated for long. Read your policy before the next winter.

Cold snaps and pipes in Duluth

Gwinnett County sees freezing nights several times most winters, and an occasional deep freeze. Homes built in the 1980s and 1990s often have supply lines in exterior walls and unheated garages. The county's water utility reminds customers that the owner is responsible for the line from the meter to the house.

Know where your main shutoff is before a freeze. The valve at the meter belongs to the county, so use the one inside your house. We serve all of Duluth and nearby Peachtree Corners.

According to the National Weather Service, dripping faucets, open cabinet doors and pipe insulation are the basic steps to protect plumbing in a hard freeze.
