What we do

Duluth 24/7 Plumbing handles drains, sewer lines, water heaters, leaks, burst pipes, toilets, faucets and commercial plumbing for homes and businesses in Duluth and nearby parts of Gwinnett County. Call (770) 214-4545 to talk through your problem.

We take calls around the clock because plumbing failures do not wait for business hours. The line is answered every day, including nights, weekends and holidays, and you can ask about timing for emergencies when you call.

How we work

These are the habits we hold ourselves to on every call.

Answer first

The call starts with questions about what you see and hear, so the plumber is prepared.

Price before work

You hear the price and the plan before the work begins, and any change is explained first.

Show the problem

We point out the failed part or the damaged pipe so you can see why the repair is needed.

Explain the options

Repair, replace or wait are laid out with the trade-offs.

Use clear language

No jargon, and no pressure.

Clean up

We leave the work area tidy.

Local knowledge

Duluth grew from fewer than 3,000 residents in 1980 to more than 31,000 in 2020, according to Census counts, and much of that housing was built in the 1980s and 1990s. That shapes the work. Water heaters are on their second or third round, supply lines are aging and sewer laterals have had decades to collect roots.

We learn each part of the city's plumbing from the job itself, and we read the county and city guidelines so that what we tell you matches the local rules. Gwinnett County Water Resources supplies water and runs the public sewer, and we explain where county responsibility stops and yours begins.

What a visit looks like

A plumber arrives, listens to what you have seen and heard and then looks at the problem before offering a price. If the work is simple, it may be finished in the same visit. If it needs parts we do not carry, we tell you when we can return and what the temporary fix is.

We explain each step in plain language and answer your questions. When the work is done, we test it, clean up and show you how to keep it working. We also tell you what to watch for, such as a slow drain that may return or a part that is near the end of its life.

Who we serve

Our customers are homeowners, renters who call on behalf of a landlord, property managers and small businesses. A homeowner may need a fast repair on a Sunday, and a manager may need a plan for a building with several units. A small business may need both at once. We take both calls the same way, by listening first and then explaining the options.

We also work with people who are not sure what is wrong, and with neighbors who simply want a second opinion on a quote they received. If you can describe what you see and hear, we can usually tell you what it is and how urgent it is before a plumber arrives.

Licensing and permits

Georgia licenses plumbers through the Secretary of State's plumbers division, and you can check any plumber's license on the state's lookup tool before you hire. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Duluth permits and inspections office what applies to your project. We pull permits where they are required.

If you want to verify anything about us, call and ask. We would rather answer a question than lose your trust.

Georgia's state plumbing code is based on the International Plumbing Code, and the work we do is built around it. When a rule affects your project, such as a relief valve, a vent or a cleanout, we explain what it requires and why it exists.
