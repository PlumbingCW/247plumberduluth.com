Privacy Policy

This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

This website has no forms. When you call us, we collect the phone number you call from and the details you share about your problem and your address, so we can arrange service and follow up on the visit.

Like most websites, the server may log technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit. We use it to keep the site running and to understand how it is used.

How we use information

We use the information you give us to schedule and complete plumbing work, to confirm details and to answer questions. We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with people who help us do the work, such as a plumber or a call-handling service, and where the law requires it.

Your choices and contact

You can ask us what information we hold about you, and you can ask us to correct or delete it, subject to legal requirements. Contact us at contact@247plumberduluth.com or call (770) 214-4545.

We may update this policy from time to time, and the latest version is always the one on this page.

Where We Serve Around Duluth

Our service area centers on Duluth along Pleasant Hill Road and Peachtree Industrial Boulevard and reaches west toward Peachtree Corners.
