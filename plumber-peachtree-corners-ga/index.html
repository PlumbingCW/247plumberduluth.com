Emergencies we answer in Peachtree Corners

These are the calls that cannot wait, and we treat each one as urgent.

Burst or leaking pipe

Shut off the main valve, then call. We repair the break and restore water. See burst pipe repair.

Sewage backup

If a tub or a floor drain fills with waste, stop using water and call. We clear the line and check the pipe. See sewer line cleaning.

Overflowing toilet

Close the valve behind the toilet and call us for a repair at any hour. See toilet repair.

No hot water

A failed tank or a tripped unit leaves the house cold. We repair or replace it. See water heater repair.

Water pouring from the heater

Turn off the power or gas, close the cold inlet and call. See water heater replacement.

Meter running with everything off

A hidden leak is draining your water bill. See leak detection.

Prepare before the plumber arrives

A few minutes of preparation limits damage and speeds the visit.

Find the main valve

Close it if water is escaping. Most homes have it in a garage, a utility room or a basement.

Clear the work area

Move rugs and furniture and clear a path to the water heater, the sink or the cleanout.

Turn off power to wet areas

Do this from a dry spot, and stay away from standing water near outlets.

Take photos

Capture the damage for insurance before you start cleaning.

Keep pets and children away

Sewage and hot water are hazards.

Have your phone ready

We may call you from the road to confirm the address and the symptoms.

Peachtree Corners homes and what they tend to need

Residential development here began in the late 1960s under the planned community concept, and the 1970s brought neighborhoods such as Peachtree Station, Chattahoochee Station and Spalding Corners. That means many homes are 40 to 55 years old, with original supply lines, older water heaters and cast iron or clay drains that have had decades to age.

Those systems are where most of our calls start. A rusted supply line leaks at a joint, an old tank gives out and a sewer lateral with roots or a belly backs up. We check the pipe material and explain what we find. If the lateral is the cause, sewer line repair is the lasting fix, and whole-house repiping replaces worn supply lines.

Businesses in Technology Park and along Peachtree Parkway

The city has a large business and technology district, including Technology Park Atlanta and the Curiosity Lab smart city campus. Offices, restaurants and retail spaces here rely on working restrooms, water heaters and drains, and an outage can close the doors for the day.

We provide commercial plumbing for Peachtree Corners businesses, including emergency calls at night and on weekends. Tell us whether the building is open when you call so we can plan the safest way to work.

Water, sewer and rules in Peachtree Corners

Gwinnett County Water Resources supplies water and operates the public sewer in this part of the county. The owner is responsible for the private sewer lateral and for the water line from the meter to the house, and the county handles the meter and the public mains. We tell you which side of the line a problem is on.

Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Peachtree Corners or Gwinnett County about your project. We pull permits where they are required and tell you before the work begins. For a planned project such as water heater installation, we can discuss timing and rules on the call.
