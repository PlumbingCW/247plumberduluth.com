Emergency Response

What Happens When You Call Our 24/7 Line

A plumbing emergency in Duluth tends to arrive at an awkward hour. A supply line splits behind a wall on a Saturday night, a main sewer line backs up on a holiday or a water heater starts to leak before the morning commute. Our line is open around the clock for exactly those calls.

When you call, we ask where you are, what you see and whether water is still running. If it is, we tell you how to shut off the main valve. Then we line up a plumber with the parts and equipment that match what you described. Our burst pipe repair and drain cleaning services handle the two calls we get most.

Once we arrive, we find the cause before we quote. You hear the price and the plan before the work begins, and any change is explained first. We show you the failed part, so that the repair makes sense to you.

Water Heaters

Water Heater Problems In Duluth Homes

Duluth grew from fewer than 3,000 residents in 1980 to more than 22,000 by 2000, according to Census counts, and most of that housing was built in those decades. Water heaters in those homes are now on their second or third unit, and many are at the age where failure arrives without warning.

A tank that leaks from the body needs water heater replacement. A heater that runs but gives lukewarm water often needs water heater repair, such as a new element or thermostat. If you are choosing between a tank and an on-demand unit, our tankless water heater page explains the trade-offs.

For planned upgrades, water heater installation covers moving a unit, changing fuel and adding venting.

Drains And Sewer

Drain And Sewer Backups Around Duluth

Gwinnett County Department of Water Resources runs the public sewer, and the county's guidelines put the private lateral from your house to the connection on the owner. When a main line backs up, the question is whether the blockage is on your side. Our plumbers tell you where it sits.

A simple clog is cleared with a cable. A line that keeps clogging may need hydro jetting or a camera, and a cracked or collapsed pipe needs sewer line repair. Large trees and aging pipes make roots a common cause in older Duluth neighborhoods.

The county also asks households to keep fats, oils and grease out of drains. That one habit prevents many of the backups we clear.

Leaks And Water Bills

Hidden Leaks And High Water Bills

A meter that moves when everything is off, a bill that jumps or a stain that spreads on a ceiling all point to a leak. Gwinnett County describes a simple meter test, and we use it as the first step. Then pressure testing and acoustic tools find the exact spot, so we open as little as possible.

See leak detection for the full process, and water line repair if the break is on the line between the meter and the house. For a slab or wall leak, we explain the options and the price before any repair begins.

Local Knowledge

Plumbing In A City That Grew Fast

Duluth sits in northern Gwinnett County, with the Chattahoochee River forming its northern boundary. Its census counts show how quickly it filled in, from 2,956 residents in 1980 to 9,029 in 1990 and 22,122 in 2000. That pace shaped the plumbing, because so many homes were built within the same short period.

Those homes now share a set of aging parts: original shutoff valves that seize, supply lines that corrode and sewer laterals with roots. We replace worn parts as we find them, and we explain what is urgent and what can wait. For businesses along Pleasant Hill Road and Peachtree Industrial Boulevard, our commercial plumbing service covers restrooms, kitchens and water heaters.
