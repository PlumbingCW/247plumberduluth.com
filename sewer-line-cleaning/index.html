A main line clog is not a small clog

A sink clog affects one sink. A main line clog affects the whole house, and the lowest drain, usually a tub, a shower or a basement floor drain, is the first to fill. Water from the washer may rise in the tub. Toilets gurgle and bubbles show up in drains elsewhere.

That pattern means wastewater has nowhere to go. We answer 24/7 for it, and the plumber starts by finding the cleanout, which is the capped pipe that gives direct access to the main line.

Stop using water and do not flush. Each use adds to the volume that is trying to leave, and it may push sewage into the lowest fixture.

What blocks a main line

The cause matters because it decides the equipment.

Tree roots

Fine roots enter through joints and catch paper and waste.

Grease and fat

Fat cools and hardens on pipe walls and narrows the line over time.

Wipes and flushed items

Products labeled flushable often do not break down.

Scale and sediment

Older pipe collects scale that snags debris.

A belly in the line

A low spot holds water and waste and clogs repeatedly.

Foreign objects

Toys and hygiene products can lodge in the pipe.

How we clear a main sewer line

The method is chosen for the clog, not by habit.

  • 1Locate the cleanout, or pull a toilet if the house has none.
  • 2Run a sewer cable with the right cutter head to open the line and cut roots.
  • 3Flush the line with water to confirm free flow.
  • 4Camera the pipe when the cause is unclear or the clog returns.
  • 5Recommend jetting or repair only when the evidence supports it.

When cleaning should lead to a bigger fix

If a line clears and backs up again within a few weeks, the cause is still in the pipe. Hydro jetting cleans the whole wall of the pipe and is the usual next step for grease and heavy roots. A sewer camera inspection shows the pipe itself.

If the camera finds a crack, an offset or a belly, cleaning only buys time. Sewer line repair fixes the damaged section, and we show you the footage so you can judge for yourself.

A cleanout installed at the right spot makes all future service faster and cheaper. If your house lacks one, we can add it during the visit.

Cleaning main lines in Duluth

Gwinnett County states that the owner is responsible for the private lateral up to and including the connection to the public sewer, so a clog inside that line is yours to clear. If a clog is in the public main in the street, the county handles it, and our plumber will tell you which side holds the blockage.

County guidelines also ask households to keep fats, oils and grease out of drains, and to put wipes in the trash. Both habits cut the number of main line clogs we see. We serve Duluth and nearby Peachtree Corners, including homes and small businesses in every Duluth ZIP code from 30095 to 30099.

Preventing the next main line backup

Keep grease out of the kitchen drain. Pour cooled cooking fat into a can and put it in the trash, and wipe greasy pans with a paper towel before washing. Gwinnett County asks households to follow the same practice.

Flush only paper and waste, and put wipes, hygiene products and dental floss in the trash. Watch the trees near the sewer line, because roots grow toward the moisture that escapes at joints.

If your house has had two backups in a year, ask for a camera check. The footage shows the cause, and a targeted repair is cheaper than repeated emergency cleaning.

Codes and standards behind the work

The International Plumbing Code (IPC) requires accessible cleanouts on building sewers, which is why we look for one first. According to Gwinnett County guidelines, wipes and grease belong in the trash and not in the drain, and following them prevents many main line clogs.
