When a backup calls for jetting right away

A main line that backs into a tub, a shower or a floor drain needs attention today. We take those calls 24/7, and if a cable can open the line fast we start there. If the plumber sees heavy buildup, repeated failures or roots, jetting is the better fix, and we can talk through scheduling when you call.

While you wait, stop using water, and keep family and pets away from any area where sewage is backing up. Do not use chemical cleaner in a backup. It will not clear a heavy clog and it makes the next job riskier.

If sewage has spread inside, keep the area closed and call us. Contact surfaces carry bacteria, and we advise on safe cleanup steps.

How hydro jetting works

A hose with a special nozzle is fed into the line through a cleanout. Water sprays backward and forward at high pressure, which cuts grease, scale and soft roots from the pipe wall and pushes the debris downstream and out. Unlike a cable, which punches a hole through the middle of a clog, jetting cleans the whole circumference of the pipe.

We use a camera before and after on most jetting visits. The first pass shows the buildup, the root intrusion and any damaged section. The second confirms the line is clear and tells you whether a repair is needed.

Jetting is not right for every pipe. It should not be used on a line that is collapsed, badly offset or made of very fragile material, and the camera tells us that before we start.

Why jetting suits many Duluth lines

Duluth grew quickly between 1980 and 2000 according to Census counts, so a lot of its sewer laterals have been in the ground for 25 to 45 years. Over that time roots find joints, and grease and scale coat the walls. Restaurants along the commercial corridors are also major sources of grease in shared lines.

Gwinnett County runs a grease management program for restaurants and other food service businesses, and tells homeowners to keep fats and oils out of drains. The county also states that the owner is responsible for the entire private sewer lateral, up to and including the connection to the public sewer. If your lateral clogs, the cleaning is on you, and jetting is often the most effective option.

We jet lines at homes and commercial sites across Duluth and in Peachtree Corners. For ongoing grease issues in a commercial kitchen see commercial plumbing.

Codes and standards behind the work

Hydro jetting is a recognized drain cleaning method in the plumbing trade, and the International Plumbing Code (IPC) sets the standards for building sewers that the process is meant to keep clear. According to Gwinnett County, owners are responsible for their private laterals, so jetting is often an owner decision.
